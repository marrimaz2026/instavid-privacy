InstaVid Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-03 · Version 1.0.0

Summary

InstaVid works entirely inside your browser. It has no server, no account, no analytics, and it does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.

What InstaVid reads

  • On www.instagram.com, when you open the extension, it reads the page you are viewing to find the video on screen, the original poster's username, the publication time and the video file address.
  • To read those details, it may request the post's own page from www.instagram.com (the same address you are already viewing), exactly as your browser would. That request goes only to Instagram and uses your normal browser session; InstaVid never reads, stores or copies your cookies, password or login details.

What InstaVid stores

  • One preference (whether to use a Downloads sub-folder), saved with chrome.storage.local on your device.
  • Nothing else. Post details are held in memory only while needed, and are not saved.

What InstaVid does with the video

InstaVid asks Chrome's Downloads feature to save the video file from Instagram's own media servers to your computer. The file is not routed through any other server.

What InstaVid does not do

  • It does not collect, sell, share or transmit personal data, browsing history, or post details.
  • It does not ask for your Instagram password and does not log in on your behalf.
  • It does not bypass private accounts, paywalls, DRM or other access controls. It can only use video files that Instagram already exposes to the account you are signed in with.
  • It does not run remote code, and uses no third-party libraries or trackers.

Permissions

  • downloads — save the video to your computer.
  • storage — remember your one preference.
  • https://www.instagram.com/* — read the post you are viewing, as described above.

Your responsibility

Videos on Instagram may be protected by copyright and by Instagram's Terms of Use. Only download content you own or have permission to save, and use downloads in line with the law and the creator's rights.
